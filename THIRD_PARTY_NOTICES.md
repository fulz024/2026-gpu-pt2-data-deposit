# Attribution and reuse

The authors' plotting and verification code and documentation retain the
BSD 3-Clause license in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The numerical result tables were curated for the associated manuscript.
Published comparison values retain their source links and model definitions
in `data/figure5_literature.csv`. Molecular inset coordinates are attributed
in `plot_settings.json` to the supporting data for DOI
10.1021/acs.jctc.4c00092 and DOI 10.1021/acs.jctc.9b01255. Third-party materials
retain their original source terms.
